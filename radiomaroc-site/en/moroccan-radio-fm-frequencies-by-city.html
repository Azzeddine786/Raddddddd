FM Frequencies of Moroccan Radio Stations by City: Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier

By: the Radio Maroc editorial teamPublished: November 20, 2026Updated: November 20, 2026Reading time: 5 min
In this article
  1. Confirmed frequencies
  2. How to find a station’s frequency where you live
  3. Stations by city

FM frequencies differ from city to city and can change over time, which is why we only publish a number after verifying it with an official source. This page lists the stations by city, a table of confirmed frequencies, and reliable ways to find the frequency in your own city.

Confirmed frequencies

We do not publish an FM frequency until we have verified it with an official source, because frequencies differ from city to city and can change. To find the frequency in your city: check the station’s official website or social accounts, let your car radio scan for stations (it shows their names), or look at the frequency plans published by the HACA, Morocco’s audiovisual regulator. Listening online from this page needs no frequency at all.

How to find a station’s frequency where you live

  1. The station’s official website or official social accounts, which often list frequencies by city.
  2. Your car radio: turn on automatic scanning; many sets show station names instead of numbers.
  3. Frequency plans published by the HACA, Morocco’s audiovisual regulator.
  4. Listening online from our directory, which needs no frequency at all.
