French-Language Radio in Morocco: The Best Stations to Listen to Live

By: the Radio Maroc editorial teamPublished: November 20, 2026Updated: November 20, 2026Reading time: 4 min
In this article
  1. Stations that broadcast in French
  2. How to choose
  3. A tip if you are learning French
  4. Listening from France or Europe

French has a strong presence in Moroccan radio, either as the full language of the broadcast or mixed with Arabic and Darija. This guide gathers the stations in our directory that broadcast in French and what to expect from each.

Stations that broadcast in French

  • Medi 1 RadioTangier · News · Arabic and French
  • Medi 1 FrançaiseTangier · Music · French
  • Hit RadioCasablanca · Music · Arabic and French
  • Radio MarsCasablanca · Sports · Arabic and French
  • Med RadioCasablanca · Community · Arabic and French
  • Atlantic RadioCasablanca · News · French and Darija
  • URADIOCasablanca · Music · Arabic and French
  • Radio 2MCasablanca · National · French and Arabic
  • Radio YabiladiRabat · Music · Arabic and French
  • Radio PlusCasablanca · Music · Arabic and French
  • Skyrock CasablancaCasablanca · Music · French
  • Radio TangermedTangier · Regional · Arabic and French

How to choose

  • News in two languages: Medi 1 Radio in Arabic and French, and Atlantic Radio in French and Darija.
  • Music: Hit Radio, and the Medi 1 Française channel for French songs.
  • Talk and society: Med Radio.

A tip if you are learning French

Start with a station that mixes Darija and French, then move gradually to a fully French one. Radio is an excellent way to train your ear to the natural rhythm of a language.

Listening from France or Europe

Most Moroccan stations broadcast worldwide. Our guide to listening to Moroccan radio online explains how, and the guide for listeners abroad (in Arabic) covers time differences and regional restrictions.
