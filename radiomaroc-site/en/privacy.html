Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the “Radio Maroc” website (https://radiomaroc.live) may process, why, who it is shared with and how you can control it. It is written to be consistent with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data.

1. Who is responsible for data processing?

The site is run by the independent “Radio Maroc” editorial team. For any privacy question write to contact@radiomaroc.live.

2. What data may we process?

a. Data we do not collect

The site does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, address or phone number simply because you listen or browse.

b. General technical data

When you visit any website, the server receives technical information as a matter of how the internet works, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date of the request. This information is processed by the hosting provider for security and technical purposes.

c. Your messages to us

If you email us, we keep your address and the content of your message for as long as needed to reply and handle your request, then delete them when no longer needed.

d. Local storage on your device

We use the browser’s local storage (localStorage) to save your favorite stations and your choice about the cookie notice. This information stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be deleted at any time from your browser settings.

3. Legal basis and purposes

  • Running and securing the site: our legitimate interest in keeping it working and protected.
  • Replying to your messages: carrying out your request or our legitimate interest in communicating with you.
  • Local preferences: saving favorites to make the site easier to use.
  • Advertising (when enabled): your consent where the law requires it, notably for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom.

4. Cookies and advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. In that case:

  • Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.
  • Google may use an advertising cookie to show personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads in Google’s ad settings, or read how Google uses data.
  • You can also visit aboutads.info to control advertising cookies from other companies.

For full details of the types we use and how to manage them, see the cookie policy.

5. External services

The site relies on third parties that may receive some technical data (such as your IP address) when resources are requested from them:

  • The hosting provider: to serve the site’s pages.
  • Google Fonts: to load fonts.
  • jsDelivr: to load the stream-playback library (hls.js) when needed.
  • Stations’ streaming servers: when you press “Listen”, your browser connects directly to the station’s server, which has its own privacy policy that we do not control.
  • Logo sources: station logos may be loaded from their own sites or public sources.
  • Google Play: if you choose to download our app, Google’s store policy applies.

6. Retention

We keep incoming messages no longer than needed to reply and keep a reasonable record. Local-storage data stays on your device until you delete it.

7. Transfers outside your country

Some technical data may be processed by providers outside Morocco or outside the European Union. We aim to work with providers that offer appropriate data-protection safeguards.

8. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if wrong, or deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing;
  • ask for a copy of your data in a portable format where applicable;
  • lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: Morocco’s National Commission for the Control of Personal Data Protection (CNDP), or the data protection authority in your country of residence.

To exercise these rights write to contact@radiomaroc.live; we will reply within a reasonable time not exceeding 30 days.

9. Children

The site is aimed at a general audience and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you learn that a child has sent us data, write to us so we can delete it.

10. Security

We use an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and keep the data we process to a minimum. Total security cannot be guaranteed on the internet, so we recommend protecting and updating your device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the “last updated” date at the top of the page. We suggest you review it regularly.

12. Contact

For any privacy question: contact@radiomaroc.live, or through the Contact page.
