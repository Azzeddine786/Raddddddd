About Radio Maroc

“Radio Maroc” is an independent editorial directory that brings Moroccan radio together: popular and modern music, news and sports, culture and talk, religious programs, Quran and Amazigh stations. We are not affiliated with any station and are not paid to list them; we write our guides ourselves. Read more on the About page.

Frequently asked questions

How do I listen to a Moroccan radio station live?

Search for the station by name or pick a category above, then press “Listen”. The player appears at the bottom of the page and you can pause or close it at any time. No account is needed.

Is listening free?

Yes, the directory is completely free. The stream comes from the stations’ own servers and you only use your own internet data, roughly 14 to 58 MB per hour depending on stream quality.

Why does the stream sometimes stop or fail?

Streaming depends on each station’s server and on your connection. Try another station, reload the page and, if the problem continues, tell us so we can fix the link. Our guide to stream cut-outs has ten practical steps.

Can I listen from outside Morocco?

Usually yes. Most stations broadcast worldwide, although a few restrict streaming by region for rights reasons.

How do I save my favorite stations?

Press the save icon on the station card. Favorites are stored only on your device, in the browser, and appear under the “Favorites” filter.

How do I suggest a station or report a broken link?

Write to us through the Contact page with the station name, city and link, and we will check it before adding or fixing it.
