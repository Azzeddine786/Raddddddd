Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page explains what cookies and similar technologies are, how “Radio Maroc” uses them and how you can control them. It supplements the privacy policy.

What are cookies?

They are small text files that the browser stores on your device when you visit a site, helping the site remember your preferences, measure usage or show ads. There are also similar technologies such as local storage (localStorage) and device identifiers.

What do we use?

1. Essential and preferences (from the site itself)

  • rm_favs (local storage): saves your list of favorite stations on your device only.
  • rm_cookie (local storage): remembers your choice about the cookie notice so we do not repeat it.
  • rm_promo (local storage): remembers that you closed the phone-app notice.

These items are not sent to our servers and are not used to track you.

2. Third-party files

  • Google AdSense ads (when enabled): Google and its partners use cookies and identifiers to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and may personalise ads to your interests.
  • Fonts and libraries: your browser may connect to services such as Google Fonts and jsDelivr to load resources, and these services may receive technical data such as your IP address.
  • Streaming servers: when you play a station, your device connects to the station’s server, which may apply its own policies.

Consent

On your first visit a notice tells you about the use of these technologies. For visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, when advertising is enabled we rely on an approved consent tool that lets you accept, reject or customise advertising cookies, and you can change your choice at any time.

How do you control cookies?

  • From your browser: all major browsers let you block or delete cookies (in privacy and security settings).
  • Delete local storage: clear the site’s data in your browser settings to remove your favorites and preferences.
  • Personalised ads: switch them off in Google’s ad settings or at aboutads.info.

Note that blocking some files may disable features such as saving favorites.

Changes to the policy

We may update this page when the tools we use change, and will change the last-updated date at the top of the page.

Contact

For any question: contact@radiomaroc.live.
