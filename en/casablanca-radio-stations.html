Casablanca Radio Stations: The Main Stations and How to Listen Live

Casablanca is home to many private radio stations, from music to sports, talk and news. Here are the stations tied to the city in our directory and what sets each one apart.

Casablanca stations in the directory

  • Hit RadioCasablanca · Music · Arabic and French
  • Radio MarsCasablanca · Sports · Arabic and French
  • Med RadioCasablanca · Community · Arabic and French
  • Chada FMCasablanca · Music · Arabic and Darija
  • MFM RadioCasablanca · News · Arabic and Darija
  • Radio AswatCasablanca · Community · Arabic and Darija
  • Atlantic RadioCasablanca · News · French and Darija
  • URADIOCasablanca · Music · Arabic and French
  • Radio 2MCasablanca · National · French and Arabic
  • Radio PlusCasablanca · Music · Arabic and French
  • Skyrock CasablancaCasablanca · Music · French

Choose by what you want

  • Sports: Radio Mars.
  • Modern music: Hit Radio and URADIO.
  • Songs in Darija: Chada FM.
  • Talk and society: Med Radio and Radio Aswat.
  • French: Atlantic Radio.

What about the frequency?

If you are in Casablanca and want the FM frequency, see our guide to frequencies by city. To listen from your phone, the “Listen” button is all you need.
