Free, no sign-up

Atlantic Radio is a Casablanca-based station broadcasting in French and Darija, filed under News in our directory. It is a good choice if you prefer listening in French with a Moroccan tone.

What will you hear?

Try the stream to judge the station’s tone, and check its schedule on the official website, because it changes from season to season. For French-language listeners, see our guide to French-language radio in Morocco.

Best for: Listeners who like a blend of French and Darija and follow the news in a relaxed tone.

FM frequency

We do not publish an FM frequency until we have verified it with an official source, because frequencies differ from city to city and can change. To find the frequency in your city: check the station’s official website or social accounts, let your car radio scan for stations (it shows their names), or look at the frequency plans published by the HACA, Morocco’s audiovisual regulator. Listening online from this page needs no frequency at all.

How to listen

  • On a phone or computer: press “Listen” above, no account needed.
  • In the car: pair it by Bluetooth or use an AUX cable, or look up the frequency in your city in the frequency guide.
  • From abroad: most stations broadcast worldwide; details in the guide to listening online.

Frequently asked questions

How do I listen to Atlantic Radio live?

Press “Listen” at the top of this page and the stream starts in your browser, with no account or sign-up. If it does not start, reload the page or see the guide to stream cut-outs (in Arabic).

What is the FM frequency of Atlantic Radio?

Frequencies vary from city to city and can change, so we only publish a number once it is verified with an official source. Check the station’s official website or our guide to frequencies by city. Listening online needs no frequency.

Which languages does Atlantic Radio broadcast in?

Atlantic Radio is a station based in Casablanca that broadcasts in French and Darija; our directory files it under “News”.
