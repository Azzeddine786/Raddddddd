Contact Us

We would love to hear from you. Whether you want to suggest a new station, report a broken link or correct a detail, we read every serious message.

Email: contact@radiomaroc.live

How can we help?

  • Suggest a station: send the station name, city and the link to its website or stream.
  • Report a broken link: tell us the station, your device, your browser and what you saw.
  • Correct a detail: attach the source if you can so we can check and fix it.
  • Station and brand owners: to request changes to details, or the removal of a logo or station.
  • Privacy and your data: to exercise your rights of access, correction or deletion.
  • Advertising and partnerships: we welcome proposals that fit the directory’s independence.

How do we handle your message?

We usually reply to serious messages within 48 to 72 working hours, and requests that need checking may take longer. Your message stays confidential and we share it with nobody unless the law requires it. For details see the privacy policy.

Before you write

  • If there is no sound, try another station and reload the page; the problem is most often the station’s server. Our guide to listening to Moroccan radio online has fixes for most problems.
  • We are not responsible for stations’ programs or schedules. For programming questions, contact the station directly through its website.

Phone app

For feedback on the app you can use its Google Play page or write to us directly.
