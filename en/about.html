About Radio Maroc

“Radio Maroc” is an independent guide that brings Moroccan radio together in one place and writes about it in ways that help listeners: where to start, what to pick, and how to get past streaming problems. The project began with a simple idea: listening to a Moroccan station should be easy and quick, and come with written content that explains and guides rather than just a list of links.

Our mission

To bring Moroccan listeners, in Morocco and abroad, closer to the voices of their country, and to offer a clear reference on Morocco’s radio landscape. We believe radio is still a close and influential medium, and that Morocco’s linguistic and cultural diversity, from Arabic and Darija to Amazigh and French, deserves a guide that celebrates it.

What we offer

  • A station directory: a list of Moroccan stations and specialised channels, with search, categories, favorites and live listening.
  • Articles and guides: to help you pick the right station, understand the history of radio, and listen online without trouble.
  • A phone app: a dedicated Android version available on Google Play.

Our editorial approach

  • Independence: we are not affiliated with any station and are not paid to list one or to put it ahead of another.
  • Originality: we write our content ourselves and do not copy it from other sites.
  • Accuracy: we rely on public and official sources and on information from the stations, and we flag the limits of what we know when something is approximate. We do not publish an FM frequency until we have verified it.
  • Transparency: our lists are editorial and do not claim to measure audiences.
  • Usefulness first: we write to help listeners, not to stuff pages with keywords.

How do we choose and check stations?

We list stations that broadcast Moroccan content or content aimed at Moroccan listeners, we test the stream links to make sure they work, and we review the station’s description on its official site where possible. If a link stays down for a long time we fix or remove it. We do not accept illegal or offensive content.

Corrections policy

Mistakes happen in any human work. If you find inaccurate information, a link that does not work, or a logo that should change, write to us through the Contact page. We review every serious message, correct errors quickly and update the article date when a change is substantial.

Advertising and funding

The site is free for listeners. We may show ads to cover running and development costs. We keep ads clearly separate from editorial content and do not let them affect our choices. For details, read the privacy policy.

Station and brand rights

We respect station owners’ rights. Names and logos belong to their owners and are shown for identification only. See the disclaimer.

Contribute

Your suggestions make a better guide: suggest a station, report a broken link, or tell us a topic you would like us to cover. Write to us at contact@radiomaroc.live.
